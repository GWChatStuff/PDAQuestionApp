Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone or iPad Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → PDA Question → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium access until the end of your billing period.

What's included in PDA Question Premium?

Premium ($0.99/month after a 7-day free trial) unlocks AI-powered question answering, Professional Letters, Partner Sync, History, Sibling Support, Schedule Builder, and Popular Questions.

What features are free?

Emergency Scripts, PDA Dictionary, PDA Topics, Success Stories, Activity Library, and Helpful Products are always free — no subscription required.

What is Pathological Demand Avoidance (PDA)?

PDA is a profile on the autism spectrum where everyday requests and expectations trigger intense anxiety. Standard autism strategies often make things worse for PDA children. Our app provides guidance grounded in a nervous-system-first, low-demand approach.

Is this app a replacement for professional support?

No. PDA Question provides guidance informed by PDA research and professional literature, but it is not a substitute for advice from qualified medical, psychological, or educational professionals.
