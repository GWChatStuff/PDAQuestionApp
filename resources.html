Further reading

A thoughtful place to keep learning

Books already included in the original PDA Question reading list, presented without commerce links or claims that they trained the app.

These are educational resources. Inclusion does not mean PDA Question endorses every idea in a book, or that the authors agree with one another.

The Educator’s Experience of PDA

Laura Kerbey

Perspectives on how demand sensitivity may show up in education and how school environments can reduce pressure.

PDA in the Therapy Room

Rebecca Loveridge

A therapy-focused exploration of relationship, autonomy, and adapting support for PDA experiences.

Understanding Pathological Demand Avoidance Syndrome in Children

Ruth Fidler & Phil Christie

An introduction to PDA characteristics, assessment considerations, and support approaches.

The Family Experience of PDA

Eliza Fricker & Kathryn Skelton, editors; illustrated by Ruth Burrows

Family perspectives on everyday life, relationships, siblings, and varied experiences of PDA.

Brain-Body Parenting

Dr. Mona Delahooke

A broader nervous-system lens for understanding behavior and regulation needs.

The Explosive Child

Dr. Ross Greene

A collaborative problem-solving framework for looking beyond willful behavior.

Low Demand Parenting

Amanda Diekman

Practical reflections on reducing demands and sustaining lower-pressure family life.
