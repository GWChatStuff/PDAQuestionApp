Why PDA Question was created

It began with exhaustion, love, and the experience of watching conventional advice add pressure instead of easing it.

The gap in a hard moment

The family behind PDA Question learned that behavior that looked oppositional could be better approached through anxiety, autonomy, capacity, and felt safety. But understanding that in principle did not make bedtime, transitions, school pressure, or public overwhelm simple.

The missing piece was practical: a way to describe what was happening and quickly find a compassionate explanation and words to try.

What the product is designed to provide

Understanding helps parents consider what may sit beneath behavior and what may help now. Script provides calm, natural language to say aloud, send to someone else, or use as self-talk.

How the guidance was developed

PDA Question’s general concepts, strategies, and action plans were developed by comparing recurring ideas across multiple independent educational, professional, research, and lived-experience sources, then translating those shared principles into original, practical guidance.

Clear boundaries

PDA Question is an educational support tool—not a diagnostic, medical, mental-health, clinical, legal, or emergency service. It does not replace individualized professional support.

For the child who changed the lens

This is dedicated to the child who taught their family to value connection over compliance, and to families trying to find a gentler way through hard moments.
